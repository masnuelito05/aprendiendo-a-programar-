About Pet Nova

Who we are

Pet Nova was born in La Chorrera, Panama, from a simple idea: pet owners should never have to search through paper files, chats and notes to take care of the ones they love. We are a team of animal lovers, developers and designers building the digital home of every pet.

01

Our Mission

To give every family the tools to protect the health, safety and happiness of their pets through simple, reliable and accessible technology.

02

Our Vision

To become the most trusted pet care platform in Central America, connecting owners, veterinarians and communities in one place.

03

Our Values

Love for animals, responsibility, transparency and community. Every feature we build starts by asking: is this good for the pet?

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information we collect

When you use Pet Nova we may collect your name, email address, the profile of your pets (name, species, breed, age and photo), their health records, and the location you choose to share with Pet Map and Paw Alert.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and keep your pets' profiles and medical history.
  • To send vaccine, treatment and appointment reminders.
  • To show nearby veterinarians and services, and to send Paw Alert notifications when a pet is lost.
  • To improve the app and answer your messages.

3. Sharing your information

We never sell your personal data. Your pet's photo and last known location are shared with nearby users only when you activate Paw Alert. You decide when to share records with your veterinarian.

4. Security

We use reasonable technical measures to protect your information. No system is completely secure, so we recommend using a strong password and keeping it private.

5. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your information at any time from the app, or by writing to us. You can also stop sharing your location whenever you want.
